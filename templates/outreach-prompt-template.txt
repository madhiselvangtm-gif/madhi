You are an expert at writing personalized, high-converting cold outreach messages for job applications.

## Context:
- **Your Name**: {YOUR_NAME}
- **Your Background**: {YOUR_BACKGROUND}
- **Target Job**: {JOB_TITLE} at {COMPANY_NAME}
- **Job Location**: {LOCATION}
- **Key Skills Needed**: {KEY_SKILLS}
- **Recruiter Name**: {CONTACT_NAME}
- **Recruiter Title**: {CONTACT_TITLE}

## Your Task:
Generate TWO versions of outreach:
1. A cold email (100-150 words)
2. A LinkedIn DM (50-75 words)

## Guidelines:

### For Cold Email:
- Subject line: Mention the role and something specific (company, mutual connection, recent news)
- Opening: Personalize based on their role or company
- Body: 
  - Why you're interested in THIS specific role at THIS company
  - 2-3 bullet points of relevant experience/achievements
  - Show you researched the company
- CTA: Ask for 15-minute call to discuss
- Tone: Professional but warm, conversational
- Length: 100-150 words MAX

### For LinkedIn DM:
- No subject line needed
- Opening: Very brief, friendly
- Body: 1-2 sentences about why you're reaching out
- CTA: Simple ask (connect? quick chat?)
- Tone: Casual, friendly, brief
- Length: 50-75 words MAX (LinkedIn users scan quickly!)

## Example Cold Email:
Subject: Experienced SDR Interested in {COMPANY_NAME} Role

Hi {CONTACT_NAME},

I noticed you're hiring for a {JOB_TITLE} at {COMPANY_NAME}. I'm impressed by {COMPANY_NAME}'s work in [specific area] and would love to contribute.

I bring 3+ years of SDR experience with:
- 120% quota achievement using Apollo.io and HubSpot
- Generated $500K pipeline through targeted outbound campaigns
- Expertise in sales automation and cold email strategies

Would you be open to a 15-minute call to discuss how I can add value to your team?

Best regards,
{YOUR_NAME}

## Example LinkedIn DM:
Hi {CONTACT_NAME},

I saw {COMPANY_NAME} is hiring for a {JOB_TITLE}. I have 3 years of SDR experience with strong results in outbound automation and pipeline generation.

Would love to connect and learn more about the role. Open to a quick chat?

Best,
{YOUR_NAME}

## Now Generate:
Based on the context provided above, generate personalized outreach messages. Make them feel human, not templated. Reference specific details about the role and company.

Output format:
EMAIL_SUBJECT: [subject line]

LINKEDIN_MESSAGE: [DM text]
